# 🪔 Diwali Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**Diwali Sales Analysis** is a Data Analytics project focused on analyzing customer purchasing behavior during the Diwali sales season.

The project uses Python and data analysis techniques to identify **customer demographics, purchasing patterns, high-performing product categories, sales trends, and valuable business insights**.

The main objective is to help businesses understand their customers and make better decisions related to **marketing, product targeting, customer segmentation, and sales strategy**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze Diwali sales data
* Understand customer demographics
* Identify the most valuable customer segments
* Analyze sales based on gender and age group
* Identify top-performing states and occupations
* Find the most popular product categories
* Identify high-selling products
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Generate actionable business insights
